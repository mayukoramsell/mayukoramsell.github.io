Biography

Hi! My name is Mayuko Ramsell and I am a 3rd year Graphic Design student at San José State University. I transferred to SJSU from Las Postias Community college, in which I earned an associates degree in Language Arts and Communication as well as Social and Behavioral Sciences. Although I have been drawing my whole life, my path in the Graphic Design field did not begin until entering SJSU.

I am a Japanese American, First-Generation college student. I grew up in the Bay Area and have been heavily involved in the Asian Community in my hometown here in the states through various events that celebrate Asian Heritage. I have also been heavily involved in my Japanese heritage, often visiting my hometown in Japan during the summer. As a result, many of the cultural influences reflected in my pieces tend to be related to Asian Culture.

Growing up, video games played a significant role in my creativity, which has led me to pursue a career in the video game field. Video games' ability to create an interactive and immersive experience has influenced me as an artist to pursue this world and create a world for anyone to escape into. As the world of interactive media continues to evolve as technology changes, I aim to continue to create a world for people around the world to enjoy and play through the joy of human and fantastical experiences.
